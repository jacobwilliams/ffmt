Sorting use statements, and the names in their only: lists, alphabetically ignoring case.

A group of use statements is a run of them with nothing between them: a blank line, comment, preprocessor line or any other statement ends the group, so statements in different #if branches never mix. A statement moves with its continuation lines and trailing comment. Statements that share a line with another (;) or that are protected by formatter directives stay where they are and end the group. Statements of one group that use the same module keep their order.

The names of an only: list are sorted by their local name (c => x sorts as c). A list that is not sorted yet is rewritten: on one line, for line splitting to break again, or broken at commas to fit a given width, with its continuation lines starting below the first name. A statement with a comment or preprocessor line before its last line is left alone.
