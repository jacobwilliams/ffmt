Groups tokens into statements and standalone lines.

A code statement runs from its first token to the line break that ends it, including any continuation lines and the comment, blank and preprocessor lines between them. Statements separated by ; on one line are split after the ;. Lines outside statements that hold only blanks, a comment or a preprocessor directive become items of their own. Together the items cover every token exactly once, in order.
