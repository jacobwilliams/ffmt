Whitespace rules within lines: the blanks wanted between pairs of adjacent tokens.

Each rule has a mode: WS_ADD makes sure there is at least one blank on each side (more are kept, so column alignment survives), WS_REMOVE removes the blanks, and WS_KEEP leaves them as they are. Rules only look at tokens on the same line; blanks are never added or removed next to a line break, a continuation & or a comment.

Statements where operators have other meanings are recognized: unary +/-, * in real*8, print * and (*), / in array constructors, and in data, common, namelist, save and equivalence statements. format statements are left alone.
