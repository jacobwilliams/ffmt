Letter case of keywords, dot operators, intrinsic procedures, the constants of the intrinsic modules, and kinds.

Fortran names are not reserved, so a word is only treated as a keyword where its position makes it one: the leading words of a statement (end subroutine, integer, if, else if, select case, implicit none, ...), the attributes of a declaration before ::, in/out/inout in intent(), kind=/len= in a type, then, only:, result() and bind(). A word that names something (call open(...), subroutine real, end function do) keeps its case. Changing the case of a name never changes the meaning of the code, so a misclassified word is a matter of style only.

Intrinsic procedures are recognized where they are called (followed by (, or after call), and the constants and types of iso_fortran_env and iso_c_binding wherever they are used, but not as the first word of a statement, after %, as an entity being declared, or as the name of a procedure being defined. Kinds are the values of kind=, the kind of a declaration's type (real(dp) :: x), the kind of a literal (2_int64, .true._lk, ck_'a') and exponent letters (1.0e-3).

public pure subroutine kind_part(text, kind, a, b)

Where the kind is in the text of a token of kind kind: text(a:b), or b < a if it has none. For a logical constant it is the suffix (_lk in .true._lk), for a string the prefix (ck in ck_'a'). Numbers have letters only in their exponent and kind, so they are cased whole.
