Finding the Fortran files under a directory.

Files are selected by extension. Entries are excluded by glob patterns: * and ? match within a path component, ** across components, and [abc] / [!abc] match one character. A pattern without a / is matched against an entry's name, one with a / against its path relative to the directory being searched. Links to directories are not followed.
