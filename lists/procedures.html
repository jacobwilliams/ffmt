Classify every statement and compute its depth: the number of constructs enclosing it, and how many of those are program units and procedures, so callers can choose not to indent their bodies. An opening statement is at the depth outside its construct, the statements inside are one deeper, and middle (else, contains, ...) and closing statements are at the opening depth. Non-code items get the depth of the code around them.
